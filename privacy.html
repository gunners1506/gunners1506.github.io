House book: privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

What it accesses

House book asks Google for one permission: drive.file. With it, the tool can create files in your Google Drive and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive, your email, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What it does with it

It creates a folder called "House book" and keeps one Google Doc per page of the household's own notes up to date. That is all. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for this, and its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is kept only on the household's own server, readable by one user account. No data is sent to anyone else, sold, shared, or used for advertising or to train AI models. There are no analytics or cookies.

Removing access

Revoke access at any time: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access → House book → Remove access. Deleting the "House book" folder removes everything the tool created.
